01 / Business sets direction

Build from the reason, not from the tool.

We start with the change the business needs, then work backward to the smallest, safest system that can help make it happen.

01 / The outcome

What gets better for the business?

Start with the result—not an AI feature. More time for complex work, fewer dropped handoffs, a clearer view of incoming demand: say what should change before choosing what to build.

Make the measure meaningful.
02 / The workflow

Find the friction in the real work.

Trace what people do today: the trigger, the context they need, the exceptions they handle, and the next person who depends on them. The useful opportunity is usually hiding in those joins.

Design for the messy middle.
03 / The leverage

Give AI a clear, bounded job.

Automate the repeatable part. Use an assistant to retrieve, classify, summarize, or draft where it helps. Connect the tools that already carry the work. Make the edge cases easy to see.

Useful beats impressive.
04 / The handoff

Keep judgment close to the decision.

Make the review point explicit. Show where an answer came from, who owns the next step, and when the system should stop and ask a person.

A human checkpoint is part of the design.
