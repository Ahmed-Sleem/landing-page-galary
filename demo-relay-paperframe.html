02 / A considered route

Technology should earn its place.

Relay begins with the business outcome, maps the people and handoffs around it, then makes the technology legible and accountable.

Clear owners / clear checkpoints
  1. 01

    Listen for the real outcome.

    Agree what should get easier before reaching for a tool.

  2. 02

    Map the work and the handoffs.

    See where information moves, where it stalls, and where judgment belongs.

  3. 03

    Build, review, and keep ownership.

    Start with a bounded workflow, test the exception paths, and keep the next step visible.
