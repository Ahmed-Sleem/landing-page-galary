How we work

Four steps, no surprises

Every engagement runs the same way: understand the work, prove the value on something small, ship it properly, then keep it running.

We map the work before we touch a single tool.

Two weeks of shadowing, screen recordings and interviews. We follow the process end to end, count every handoff, and find where time actually disappears — which is rarely where anyone expects.

  • Current-state process map, step by step
  • Volume, cycle time and error rate per step
  • Ranked automation candidates with effort estimates

Deliverables

  • Process mapDoc
  • Automation auditDeck
  • ROI modelSheet

Typically 1–2 weeks

We prototype the highest-value workflow first.

A working prototype on your real data, not a slide. We agree on how we will judge it before we build it — accuracy thresholds, fallback behaviour, and the exact point where a human takes over.

  • Working prototype on a real sample
  • Written evaluation criteria you sign off
  • Phased rollout plan with rollback points

Deliverables

  • Live prototypeDemo
  • Eval criteriaDoc
  • Rollout planDoc

Typically 2–3 weeks

A production build, not a demo that only works on Tuesdays.

Structured logging, retries with backoff, dead-letter queues, alerting and a runbook your own engineers can follow. Everything lives in your accounts and your repositories from day one.

  • Deployed in your cloud, your accounts
  • Monitoring, alerting and cost ceilings
  • Source code, tests and runbook handed over

We watch it, tune it, and expand it.

Models drift and processes change. Every month we review accuracy, cost and volume, adjust the thresholds, and queue the next workflow — so the system gets better instead of quietly rotting.

  • Drift and failure alerts wired to your team
  • Prioritised backlog of the next automations

Still unsure about something? Ask us directly — we answer every message ourselves, usually the same day.

The first working prototype is usually live in two to three weeks. A full production system with monitoring, alerting and a runbook takes six to ten weeks depending on how many systems it touches. We start delivering value in the first month rather than disappearing until the end.

Everything runs inside your own cloud accounts under your own credentials. We work with data processors you already have an agreement with, and we never use your data to train a shared model. If a use case requires a model we cannot host in your region, we will tell you before we start rather than after.

Anything with an API, a webhook or a database you own. That covers the usual CRM, helpdesk, ERP and data-warehouse platforms, and most internal tools. If something has no interface at all, we will say so during discovery and propose the cheapest way to give it one.
