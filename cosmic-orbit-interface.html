A considered approach

A system has to earn its place.

Start small. Make the boundary clear. Keep the people closest to the work involved in shaping it.

01 — LISTEN

Find the loop.

Map what repeats, where the exceptions live, and which decisions need a person.

02 — DESIGN

Draw the boundary.

Decide what the system can read, suggest, prepare, and hand back for review.

03 — TUNE

Make it useful.

Test with the team, watch where it stalls, and adjust the workflow around real use.
