01 / Thoughtful automation

A little less busy.
A lot more useful.

Relay brings the right kind of support to the right part of the work—from repeatable routing to knowledge and cross-tool handoffs.

01Automation

Move the repeatable work forward.

Reduce manual routing and re-entry with clear rules, sensible exceptions, and a named human owner.

  • Route common requests to the next right step
  • Keep edge cases visible instead of forcing a guess
  • Make approvals and ownership easy to see
Explore workflow automation ↗

02AI assistants

Answers that know where they came from.

Help teams find approved information, shape a useful draft, and leave the review decision with a person.

  • Search connected, approved knowledge
  • Show sources alongside the answer
  • Prepare, never silently send
Explore AI assistants ↗

03Integrations

Keep the handoff feeling effortless.

Connect the tools already holding the work so context moves with it and the next action is easier to pick up.

  • Carry key information across systems
  • Surface suggested owners and next actions
  • Let a teammate confirm before assignment
Explore integrations ↗

02 / A people-first method

Thoughtful by default.

Technology should make good work easier, not harder to own. So we keep the whole route in view—from the first request to the person accountable for what happens next.

01 / LISTEN

Start with why.

Get clear on the business outcome before choosing a tool.

02 / MAP

See the real work.

Understand the handoffs, exceptions, and context people use.

03 / BUILD

Fit the right assist.

Give automation and AI a useful, bounded job to do.

Human checkpoints are part of the design. People stay responsible for review, approval, and the moments that need judgment.OUTCOME → WORK → ASSIST → OWNER
